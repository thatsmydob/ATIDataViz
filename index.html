8 Washington D.C.s Worth of People

5.6 million people used Medicaid LTSS in 2020

Watch as we visualize this scale using a familiar reference point: the population of Washington, D.C.

700,000 people live in D.C. — multiply that by 8, and you get the number of Americans who depend on LTSS for daily support.

This isn't a niche program — it's supporting millions of families across the nation.

Small Share, Big Spending Impact

6% of Medicaid enrollees use LTSS
But account for 37% of spending

Out of 100 Medicaid enrollees, only 6 need LTSS  yet they drive spending equivalent to 37 people.

Resource-intensive population � critical policy & budget implications.

Most People Receive Care at Home

Out of every 100 LTSS users:

  • 72 live in the community
  • 24 in facilities
  • 4 use both

The U.S. has shifted toward aging in place & independence.

LTSS Is Not Only for Older Adults

56% of LTSS users are under age 65

More than half are younger adults with disabilities or complex needs.

Shatters the myth that LTSS = "nursing homes for seniors."

Home-Based Care Is Accelerating

By 2021, ~86% of LTSS users received HCBS

Out of 100 LTSS users today, 86 receive support at home.

Structural system shift � policy success & new workforce needs.
